Cookies Policy small crumbs, big transparency

We use cookies sparingly — just enough to make your visit feel familiar and to understand how our almanac is read. This page explains what we use and why.

🍪 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, count visitors, and understand which articles are most loved. We treat them like pressed flowers — delicate and useful, but never intrusive.

📋 2. Types of cookies we use

We keep our cookie jar simple. Below is a clear table of the cookies we set and their purpose:

Name Type Purpose Duration
thicket_session Essential Maintains your session while browsing Session
pref_theme Preference Remembers your display choices (e.g., contrast) 1 year
analytics_leaf Performance Aggregates page visits (anonymised) to improve content 30 days
almanac_notice Essential Hides the cookie banner after acceptance 6 months
🌿 No third‑party advertising cookies. We do not use tracking from ad networks or social media platforms. Your reading habits remain between you and the thicket.

⚙️ 3. Managing your cookies

You are always in control. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device.
  • Block all or specific cookies.
  • Delete cookies after each visit.

To adjust your settings, check the "Privacy" or "Preferences" section of your browser. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site (such as staying logged in or saving your preferences).

🔄 4. Updates & changes

We may refine this policy as our practices evolve or to comply with new regulations. Any changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
