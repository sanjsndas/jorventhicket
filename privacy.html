Privacy Policy how we tend to your data with root & branch

Your trust is the soil in which we grow. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it — always with transparency and care.

🌱 1. Information we gather

We collect only the information that helps us cultivate a better experience for you. This includes:

  • Name and email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or send us a message.
  • Usage data — such as pages visited, time spent, and referring links, to understand what our readers enjoy most.
  • Cookies — small text files that help us remember your preferences (see our Cookies Policy for details).

📬 2. How we use your information

Every piece of data is used with a specific, respectful purpose:

  • To respond to your inquiries and correspondence.
  • To send you seasonal almanac updates, if you have opted in.
  • To improve our content and website performance.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our site.
🌿 We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your privacy is as important to us as the health of the forest.

🔒 3. Data storage & security

We store your data on secure servers with industry‑standard encryption. Access is limited to essential personnel only. We regularly review our practices to ensure your information remains safe.

📧 4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply reach out to us — we will respond within 30 days.

🍪 5. Cookies & tracking

We use minimal cookies to enhance functionality and analyse traffic. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. For a full breakdown, please visit our Cookies Policy.

🔗 6. Third‑party links

Our almanac may include links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing any personal information.

📝 7. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be posted here, with the effective date noted below.
