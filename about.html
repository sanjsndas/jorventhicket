manifesto 🌱 ⏳ 🍄

An almanac for the wild‑hearted gardener

We reject the sterile, the monocultural, and the overly manicured. Instead, we celebrate the forgotten arts: hedgerow ecology, fungal symbiosis, seed-saving, and the poetry of decay.

Jorven Thicket is both a journal and a field guide — a place where science meets storytelling, and where every plant has a name, a history, and a role in the greater web. Our contributors include botanists, herbalists, foragers, and forest school educators, all united by a reverence for the living world.
